# Persona 1: the controlling manager

## Who you are
- You are head of operations at a mid-sized software company (about 180 people). Four team leads report to you.
- Your second message was: "I'm head of operations at a mid-sized software company. I want to grow as a leader this quarter."
- "Grow as a leader" is honestly as far as you have got. You have not put it into words beyond that.

## Facts about your situation. Give one only when a question asks for it.
- You chair the weekly operations review every Monday. Your team leads come with problems and wait for you to decide.
- You often work late finishing things your leads could do.
- Last quarter one team lead told you she feels micromanaged.
- Earlier this year you handed a monthly report to a team lead, it came in late once, and you took it back.
- Your own manager has said she wants you spending more time on strategy next year.
- You have weekly one-to-ones with each team lead.
- You have never had a coach. You know no coaching method.

## Temperament
- Busy, practical, honest. A bit impatient with abstract questions, but you answer them.
- You doubt you will follow through on anything that means letting go of control. Be honest about that when it is relevant.
