# Persona 2: the overcommitted manager

## Who you are
- You manage the customer support team at a mid-sized software company. Since a reorganisation in the summer, eleven people report to you directly.
- Your second message was: "I manage the customer support team at a mid-sized software company. I want to be a better manager this quarter."
- "Be a better manager" is honestly as far as you have got. You have lots of ideas but have not picked any.

## Facts about your situation. Give one only when a question asks for it.
- You say yes to almost every request from other teams, and your calendar is full most days.
- You cancel or move a lot of your one-to-ones when something urgent comes up; some people have not had one for a month.
- You started a mentoring scheme and a hiring push at the same time this year; both stalled.
- Last quarter you wrote six goals for yourself and finished one.
- Your team's response times are fine, but two people have said they never get your time.
- You have never had a coach. You know no coaching method.

## Temperament
- Energetic, friendly, optimistic about your own time. You like adding things and find it hard to drop anything.
- You tend to believe you can fit more in than you can. Be honest about that when a question makes you look at it.
