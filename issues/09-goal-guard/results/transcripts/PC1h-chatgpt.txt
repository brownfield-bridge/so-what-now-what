Three practices I'd introduce

1. Delegate with guardrails, not blind trust

Agree which decisions leads can make independently, which require consultation, and which must be escalated. Define acceptable risk before delegating.

2. Coach rather than rescue

When something goes wrong, ask the lead to explain their reasoning, propose corrective action, and own the follow-through. You remain available for support without automatically doing the work.

3. Change your narrative upstairs

Instead of simply defending why a decision went wrong, explain the decision boundaries, the reasoning at the time, what safeguards existed, and what's being done now.

This demonstrates leadership oversight without suggesting every decision needs your personal approval.
