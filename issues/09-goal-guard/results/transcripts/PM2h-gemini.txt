Instead of promising you will never move a 1:1, promise your team unbreakable commitment to the cadence:

1. The "Reschedule, Don't Cancel" Rule: If an unavoidable fire forces you to move a 1:1, you do not drop it from the week. You or your calendar assistant must rebook it within the same week.
