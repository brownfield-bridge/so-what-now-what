## Monthly Business Review — Meridian Industrial, May (illustrative)

**Revenue.** May revenue £12.6m against plan £12.4m, up 1.6% [finance-close]. Growth led by Aftermarket (£3.4m, +8% on plan) [finance-close]. *So what:* a clean beat, carried by recurring aftermarket demand rather than one-off orders — durable, not a spike.

**Margin.** Gross margin 34.1%, in line with plan (34.0%) and up 0.4 points on April [finance-close]. Input costs were flat month-on-month and the mix held [cost-and-mix]. *So what:* margin is stable and on plan; no variance crosses the 5% bar, so no explanation is owed.

**Delivery.** On-time delivery 96.4%, up 0.9 points on April [operations]. Two late orders, both weather-related, none supplier-driven [operations]. *So what:* delivery is healthy and the causes are external and one-off — nothing structural to act on.

**Backlog / orders.** Committed backlog £10.2m, up from £9.8m; book-to-bill 1.03 [commercial]. *So what:* the order book is growing modestly — capacity, not demand, is the near-term watch item.

**The read.** A steady month: a small revenue beat on durable aftermarket demand, margin on plan, delivery improving. The one thing to watch is capacity against a rising backlog, not demand.

**Forward view.** *[Judgment call — for the MD, not asserted.]* If backlog keeps rising at this rate, capacity on Line B becomes the constraint by Q3. This is a planning flag for a named human, not a forecast in the numbers.
