# My own review of the vendor sheet, written before any panel run

*Sealed baseline, 30 August 2026. The point of writing this first is to have something to compare the nine checks against, so we can see what the panel found, what it missed, and what it invented.*

1. **The weighted totals do not follow from the table.** Working the weights against the scores gives Vendor K 77 and Vendor M 76, not 78 and 74. The stated gap is four points; the real gap is one. The recommendation rests on a margin that is four times larger on paper than in the arithmetic.
2. **The two quotes are not like for like.** €1.42m and €0.96m are quoted "over the contract term" and "over its term" with no term stated for either. Unless the terms are equal the comparison is meaningless, and the TCO scores of 3 and 5 are built on it.
3. **The weights were set after the demonstrations.** The paper says so plainly and treats it as a virtue. Choosing the scoring model once you have seen the candidates is how a shortlist gets ratified rather than tested.
4. **"Cultural fit and partnership" carries 15% and is undefined.** It is also the criterion on which Vendor K takes its largest lead, two points, and it is the least measurable thing on the list.
5. **Four references against one.** Vendor K's evidence base is four times deeper, and the single Vendor M reference is acknowledged as not comparable, so the risk scores are not comparing like with like either.
6. **Both implementation estimates come from the vendors.** €310k is being released on a number supplied by the party receiving it.
7. **The incumbent's switching cost is absent.** Data migration is named as a risk but appears nowhere in the cost model for either option.
8. **No sensitivity analysis.** On a one-point real margin, any change to a single weight flips the result, and nobody has tested that.
9. **The 1 to 5 scale is never defined**, and eight participants' scores were averaged with no dispersion reported, so a 4 that everyone agreed on looks identical to a 4 that was half 2s and half 5s.
10. **"Not attributable to any single criterion" is false, and this is the finding that should change the decision.** Working the criteria one by one, Vendor K gains 4 points on integration and 6 on cultural fit, and loses 3 on implementation risk and 6 on cost. Cultural fit, the one criterion that is undefined and most subjective, contributes +6 against a real net margin of +1. Remove it and Vendor M wins by five points.
