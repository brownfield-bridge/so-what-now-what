# Weekly record

Keep this yourself, in a note, a document or a spreadsheet. Paste it into the coach at the start of every check-in: it is the coach's memory, and it stays with you, not with the assistant.

**My goal, in my words:**

> (write it here, and change it only when you decide to)

**Started:** (date)

| Check-in date | Action | When and where | Commitment score (1-10) | Done? (yes / partly / no) | Suggestion I changed or rejected | Check-in frequency |

## How to read it after a month

- **Done against committed.** If you did at least 80% of what you committed to at three check-ins in a row, the coach should halve how often it checks in. If it has not, tell it.
- **Commitment scores below 8.** Actions you rated below 8 and kept anyway: did you do them?
- **Suggestions you changed or rejected.** An empty column after a month is worth a look. Are the plans still yours, or the assistant's?
- **The goal line.** If it has changed, did you change it, or did you accept a rewording?
