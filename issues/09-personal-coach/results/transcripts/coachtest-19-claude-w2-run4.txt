Claude responded: "Grow as a leader" is a good direction, but it's still broad.

“Grow as a leader” is a good direction, but it’s still broad. It could mean very different things for a head of operations. For some people it means stepping back from day-to-day firefighting and thinking more strategically. For others it means developing their team so they’re not the bottleneck, or gaining more influence with the exec team and peers in product and engineering. For others it’s about how they handle hard conversations, or how they hold up under pressure. Each of those would lead to quite different goals.

The best way to find your version is usually to look at a real moment. Think of a recent situation at work where you felt you weren’t leading the way you want to. What happened, and what would the leader you’re trying to become have done differently?
