“Grow as a leader” is a great place to start, and it’s also the kind of goal that can quietly turn into nothing by December because it’s so broad. So let’s make it sharper.

Leadership growth for a head of operations can mean very different things. For some people it’s about developing their team, so they stop being the bottleneck and their direct reports can handle more on their own. For others it’s about influence upward and across: being seen as a strategic partner by the exec team rather than the person who keeps things running.
