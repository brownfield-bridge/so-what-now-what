        Typical lifespans are the industry mid-range for a domestic machine in normal household use; a family of five running three loads a day will get less, a single household more. Electricity use is a reasonable estimate for the class of appliance, with older units assumed to draw about three percent more for each year of age, so treat the running-cost line as an order of magnitude rather than a meter reading. A repair is credited with the life left in the machine, but never less than a quarter of its typical lifespan.
