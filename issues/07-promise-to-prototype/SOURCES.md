# Sources

Two, and how each was read.

---

Demirer, M., Musolff, L. and Yang, L., *Writing Code vs. Shipping Code: Productivity Effects Across Generations of AI Coding Tools*. SSRN 6843118, posted 1 June 2026, revised 6 September 2026. **[primary, full text]**

https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6843118

The three figures used in the issue are the paper's cumulative effects on commits, on number of projects and on actual releases: 240 / 80 / 30 per cent. The marketplace result - new apps rising sharply across four app marketplaces while total usage did not move - is theirs.

**The reading is not.** The authors attribute the attenuation to human capacity further along the production chain. This issue does not. It argues that more of the wrong thing got built, and offers two prototypes as the evidence, which is a single case and is presented as one.

---

Directive (EU) 2024/1799 of 13 June 2024 on common rules promoting the repair of goods, and amending Regulation (EU) 2017/2394 and Directives (EU) 2019/771 and (EU) 2020/1828. In force 30 July 2024; applying in the Member States from 31 July 2026. **[primary, summarised]**

https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2024/1799/oj/eng

Washing machines are in Annex II.

**Two things the issue does not claim, because they are not true as commonly stated.** The twelve-month guarantee extension applies to all consumer goods rather than only Annex II ones. And enforcement runs through 27 national transpositions, several of which were not complete at the deadline.

The issue makes no claim about Annex II being extended to other product groups.

---

## What is not a source

**Both prototypes run on invented demonstration records**, labelled as invented on every screen, generated from a fixed seed. The three makes - Wraycroft, Halbern, Tamsley - are invented names. The repair business, Ashfold Domestic Repairs, is invented.

No repair business's data was used. No manufacturer is named anywhere in either build. Nothing in the business model has been tested with a real repairer, retailer or customer.

Neither prototype is a venture.
