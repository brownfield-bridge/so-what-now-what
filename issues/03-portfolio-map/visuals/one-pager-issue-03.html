The test — four questions

The one that stays empty is the decision you never made.
1
Owner
Who owns the outcome — a business owner whose own number moves, not a systems owner and not nobody?
2
Process & skill
What process changes when it works, and what new skill does that change demand?
3
Autonomy
What may the system do alone, and what needs sign-off?
4
Success test
How will you know it works — on your benchmark, or the vendor’s?
