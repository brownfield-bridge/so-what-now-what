# Assumption: the freed team is absorbable

*Example file, referenced by `decisions/2025-09-exit-public-tender-practice.md`.*

**Assumes:** the team freed by exiting the public-tender practice is absorbed by private-sector work within two quarters, at current demand.

**Holds while:** private-sector demand stays at or above 2025 levels and nobody is reassigned long-term.

**Watch:** any long-term commitment of the freed team elsewhere, or a private-sector demand drop. (Updated by the weekly review when a `signals/` item contradicts it.)
