# This folder is the connector

The controller reads **one place**, and this is it. That single choice is why the kit works with
whatever you already use and needs nobody's permission to install.

**One rule: if you can drop it in, it counts.**

- A typed line after a call
- A note dictated on your phone
- A photograph of a page from your notebook
- A forwarded email, or a thread exported as text
- An exported chat
- Meeting notes, however scrappy, from whatever takes them for you

It does not need to be tidy, consistent, or written in sentences. The demo files here are
deliberately messy, because real material is.

**Two conventions, and they are the only two.**

1. **Put a date in the filename**, `YYYY-MM-DD-whatever.md`. The controller can usually find a
   date inside the material, but the filename is what makes ageing reliable, and ageing is the
   whole point.
2. **Anything starting with `_` is ignored.** Notes to yourself, like this file, live here safely.

**The controller reads this folder and never writes to it.** The register lives one level up.

---

## The one habit this asks of you

A phone call leaves no trace unless you make one. Everything else in your working life already
writes itself down somewhere - mail, chat, meeting notes, calendar. Calls do not.

So: **when you put the phone down, one line. Who, what, by when.** Twenty seconds, in whatever
form suits you. See `2026-08-18-call-note.md` for how little is enough.

That is the whole of the manual work.

## The demo files

Nine files, one month, a fictional Head of Operations at a mid-sized manufacturer. They contain,
on purpose:

- commitments in **both** directions
- one that **nobody has mentioned in 40 days** and that nobody ever decided to drop
- one that was **renegotiated** and never written down anywhere else
- one **vague** promise with no person and no time, which the controller refuses to turn into a row
- the **same commitment mentioned twice**, in two files, which must become one row and not two
- a commitment between **two other people** that the controller should leave alone
- a couple of things that **look** like commitments and are not

Delete them all when you have seen one run, and drop in your own.
