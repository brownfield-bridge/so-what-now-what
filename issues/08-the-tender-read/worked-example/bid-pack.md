# Worked example: the bid pack

**Entirely fictional.** No company, person, price or document here is real, and nothing in it is drawn from any actual procurement. It exists so the method can be run end to end and produce numbers, rather than having numbers written into it.

The request for quotation below is deliberately imperfect. It is written the way a real one gets written by a manager with three weeks and a day job, which is the point, because the first stage of the method is finding what it failed to ask.

---

# PART 1: THE REQUEST FOR QUOTATION

**Northvale Industrial Components Ltd: Warehouse and Dispatch Management System**
**RFQ NV-2026-114 · Issued 4 May 2026 · Responses due 12 June 2026**

## 1. Background

1.1 Northvale operates four distribution sites (Leeds, Rotterdam, Poznań, Lyon) holding approximately 11,000 active stock keeping units of industrial components.

1.2 Our current warehouse system was built in house in 2014. The two people who understood it have left the business. It cannot be extended and it is increasingly expensive to keep running.

1.3 We have committed to our three largest customers that orders received before 14:00 will be dispatched within 48 hours. We currently measure this manually and we believe we are meeting it, but we cannot evidence it consistently.

## 2. Scope

2.1 A warehouse and dispatch management system covering goods receipt, putaway, stock control, picking, packing, dispatch and returns across all four sites.

2.2 Integration with our existing ERP (finance and order management) and with three carrier systems.

2.3 Migration of current stock records, locations and open orders.

2.4 Training for approximately 120 warehouse users and 14 supervisors.

2.5 Support from go-live.

## 3. Requirements

3.1 The system must support our 48-hour dispatch commitment.

3.2 The system must give us accurate stock visibility across all four sites.

3.3 The system must support barcode scanning on handheld devices.

3.4 The system must provide reporting on warehouse performance.

3.5 The system should be capable of supporting future growth including a possible fifth site.

3.6 The system must be available during our operating hours.

3.7 Data must be held within the European Union.

## 4. Commercial

4.1 Please provide licence costs, implementation costs and annual support costs separately.

4.2 Please indicate your proposed implementation timetable.

4.3 Please state your standard contract terms.

## 5. Evaluation

5.1 Responses will be evaluated on functional fit, implementation approach, supplier capability and total cost over five years.

5.2 Northvale reserves the right not to award.
