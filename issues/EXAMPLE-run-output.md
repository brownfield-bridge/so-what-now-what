## What to notice about this run

**The first draft is the cheapest conversation of the week.** C-009 is not late, not quiet and not a problem. It is going out four days early because something was watching a date you had already stopped thinking about - and telling somebody early costs you almost nothing, while being asked late costs you a great deal. Nobody builds this half, and it is the half that makes the tool worth having on a good week.

**Nothing was raised twice.** Every row here now carries the date it was surfaced. C-011 will not appear again next Monday at 32 days; it comes back when the row changes, or when the silence doubles to 50. If you read a draft and decide not to send it, that is a decision and it is respected without comment. This one rule is what separates this from every reminder tool you have already muted.

**It found four things and refused to find four more.** The `unclear` list is not a failure. It is the mechanism working: four things in the material looked like commitments, and turning them into rows would have made the register bigger and less true.

**The rows that matter are not the newest.** The two findings are 25 and 17 days old and neither would have triggered a reminder anywhere, because C-011's date was a soft "next week or two" and C-014's tender deadline passed without an alert against the promise itself.

**C-003 is the point of the whole exercise.** It was not missed, it was not late, and nobody failed. It stopped existing. September intake is in three weeks and the deck is from 2024. **The only reason you know is that something counted the days of silence.**

**One row is the argument in miniature.** C-006 shows a date that moved by agreement. That is a decision, properly made, and it is now recorded as a decision. Everything else you own records only the new date.

**Read two places, not the whole list.** What you are owed by people who are expensive to chase - C-011, a supplier. And what you owe upwards - C-009, the board, due on the 28th and not yet quiet. Those are the two. The rest can wait a week and nothing will happen.

**And most weeks look like nothing.** This run had a month of material behind it. A normal Monday produces one finding or none, and most days produce nothing at all because it only speaks when something crosses a line. The weekly digest still arrives to say so. **When it produces nothing, that is the product working, not the product failing.**

**One question would have come with this digest.** The calendar shows a 30-minute call on 18 August that produced only a one-line note, and a 45-minute slot on 12 August that produced nothing anywhere. The second gets one question - *"Tuesday the 12th, 45 minutes, and nothing came out of it. Anything promised?"* - and one line back builds the rows. That is the habit replaced by an answer.
